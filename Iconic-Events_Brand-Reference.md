# Iconic Events LLC — Brand Reference

*Distilled from Brand Guidelines, Edition 02 (Volume 02 · 2026). Working reference for building on-brand deliverables, including the sales page.*

---

## 1. The Brand in One Line

> Iconic Events is a luxury experiential events agency whose every output reads like it was **engraved, not generated**.

**Founded:** 2017, on the conviction that the experience industry had drifted toward generic, scalable, and loud. Iconic works in the opposite register: considered, quiet, specific.

**Position:** *Heritage* luxury — not hospitality luxury. Reference points are private libraries, members' rooms, and the ritual of a well-set table. **Not** boutique hotels, not modern minimalism, not maximalist hospitality.

**Taglines in use:** "A Standard of Iconic." · "Engraved, not printed."

### The Four Pillars

| Pillar | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Considered** | Every choice has a reason. No decoration, no padding. White space is intentional. |
| **Direct** | We recommend, we don't guess. No hedges. Conviction is the brand. |
| **Heritage** | The visual reference is library, leather, brass. |
| **Operator-led** | The work is built by people who run rooms. The voice is someone who has done it before. |

## 2. Voice & Tone (the most important section for copy)

The Iconic voice is **the voice of the most prepared person in the room** — calm, declarative, specific. It does not flatter the client. It earns trust by sounding like it has done the job before.

### The Sherpa Dynamic
The client has the vision; Iconic has the route.
- Present a **recommendation**, not a menu of options.
- **Describe the next step** — don't ask permission.
- Never deferential. Never showy.

### The Certainty Filter
Every sentence must pass before it ships:

| Test | Question | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Conviction** | Are we recommending or hedging? | Strike "we could," "perhaps," "if you're open to it." |
| **Specificity** | Is the detail concrete? | Names, times, counts, addresses. No "various" or "several." |
| **Necessity** | Does the sentence earn its place? | If it could be cut without loss, cut it. |

### Weak vs. Iconic

> **Weak:** "We could probably do a tasting around 6 if that works for everyone — let me know what you think and I can put together some options."
>
> **Iconic:** "Tasting at 18:00. Three courses, paired. The chef has held the table; confirm by Friday."

> *"Words are tools. Use the sharp ones."*

## 3. Color

### Document Palette
Tide Pool leads. Onyx carries every word.

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Onyx** | `#0E1312` | Body text, table cell text, titles. House override of pure black. |
| **Tide Pool** | `#112E2E` | Section headers, subheaders, table header fill, 4pt rules, all accents. *The only green.* |
| **Tide Pool Tint** | `#DDE5E4` | Alternating rows on striped tables. |
| **Light Grey** | `#F2F2F2` | Alternating rows in non-Tide-Pool contexts. |
| **Border Grey** | `#D0D0D0` | All table cell borders. |
| **Footer Grey** | `#888888` | Footer disclaimer text. |
| **Page Number Grey** | `#AAAAAA` | Footer page numbers. |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Page background, non-table fills. |

### Editorial Extension
For decks, signage, stage graphics, social, environmental — **and arguably the sales page**, since it's an editorial surface.

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Onyx** | `#0E1312` | Editorial dark surface. Replaces black. |
| **Tide Pool** | `#112E2E` | Primary accent across every surface. |
| **Antique Brass** | `#B89968` | Sigil on dark, seals, flourishes, section ordinals. **Never a body fill.** |
| **Bone** | `#F4F0E8` | Warm paper tone. Replaces white on editorial surfaces. |

**Rules:**
- Tide Pool is the only green used anywhere.
- **One accent per view** — Tide Pool leads *or* Antique Brass leads, never both at the same weight.
- Brass appears only in small, deliberate moments — a sigil, a numeral, a hairline rule.
- Antique Brass and Bone are **not** permitted inside client documents (but are correct for editorial surfaces).

## 5. Logo System

A serif wordmark with a vertical **IE sigil** at center. Two configurations, two contexts.
- **Wordmark** for identification.
- **Sigil** where the brand is already established.
- **Never** use sigil and wordmark together in the same composition.

**Files:** `logo-black.png`, `logo-white.png`, `sigil-black.png`, `sigil-white.png`

| Spec | Wordmark | Sigil |
|---|---|---|
| Clearspace | 1× cap height, all sides | 0.5× sigil width, all sides |
| Min. print | 1.0 in wide | 0.4 in tall |
| Min. screen | 120 px wide | 32 px tall |

**File use:** Black on light surfaces. White on Onyx, Tide Pool, Antique Brass, or photography.

### Misuse — never do
Distort or stretch · rotate · add shadow or outline · use off-system color · place on gradient backgrounds · place on the pattern · recolor outside Onyx/Bone/white/Antique Brass · reproduce from a screenshot.

## 6. Layout & Structure

- **Sharp corners always.** Luxury favors sharpness over softness.
- Page: US Letter, portrait, 1 in margins (document context).
- Header: full-width Tide Pool banner, white wordmark centered.
- Footer: 4pt Tide Pool rule, disclaimer left, page number right.
- Single column at 6.5 in unless a table needs full width.

## 8. How to Write On-Brand (applied cheatsheet for the sales page)

When drafting copy, run every line through this:

1. **Lead with a claim, not a question.** "We produce the room." — not "Can we help with your event?"
2. **Cut the hedges.** Delete *could, might, perhaps, we think, if you're open to it, some, various, several.*
3. **Get specific.** Real numbers, real formats, real deadlines. Specificity *is* the proof.
4. **Recommend, don't offer.** State the next step as though it's already decided.
5. **No padding.** If a sentence can be cut with no loss, cut it. Short paragraphs. Intentional white space.
6. **Heritage register.** Library, leather, brass, engraving, the well-set table. Never "cutting-edge," "solutions," "seamless," or hospitality-brochure language.
7. **One accent.** Tide Pool leads on a light/Bone surface; Antique Brass for the single deliberate flourish.
