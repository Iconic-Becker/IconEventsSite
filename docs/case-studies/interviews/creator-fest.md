## Research (2026-10-25)

The user pasted research for Creator Fest 2024. Recorded here, leaving out
the internal details the research itself flagged (early ticket numbers, a
cut talk, a travel dispute, a broken verification tool). Publishable:

- The first edition, internal codes M112 and M114. Renamed CreatorHub
  (now CreatorHub Live) after a trademark issue in October 2024.
- Why: a business first industry event for creators; fan events and video
  growth events did not cover the business side. Five pillars: product,
  distribution, management, monetization and growth.
- Partner from the start: Patrick Israel, co-founder, Stem Media Group.
  Iconic ran venue, production and speakers; Stem ran talent, sponsors and
  ticket sales. Xtend Creators appears only as a connected Slack org; the
  ownership split is not written down. (The live CreatorHub Live page says
  co-owned with Xtend Creators: confirm.)
- Decisions: GA closed the Saturday before, VIP only after, to push
  upgrades; a sponsor bought block of 100 GA tickets given away by
  speakers, 5 each; the leadership panel scrapped; the founder's opening
  reworked into a hero's journey talk with an intro video; lanyards by
  category (Creator, Industry, Production, Brand).
- Hard: little money or credibility early; a shifting speaker lineup in
  the final weeks; sponsor management handed to a new coordinator three
  weeks out.
- How it went: about 600 attendees (610 in the debrief), called the
  largest creator event on the East Coast; 70+ podcasts recorded on site;
  breakouts running 45 minutes to 3.5 hours; praise from Snapchat's
  representative and former VidCon CEO Jim Louderback; about 7 TB of
  content. Lessons carried into 2025: start and lock sponsors earlier,
  10+ volunteers, venue access the day before, better registration and
  badges, a year round alumni community, virtual tickets, an event app, an
  ambassador program, scaling toward 1,000.
- Facts: August 15 to 18, 2024, JW Marriott Orlando Bonnet Creek Resort &
  Spa, Orlando, FL. Four event days. Cost saved not confirmed.
- Speakers: Jim Louderback, Brooke Berry (Snapchat), Tyler Blanchard,
  Eddie Maalouf, Kelsey Tjernlund, Andy Morris, Aylex Thunder.
- Links: aftermovie on Google Drive (needs YouTube to embed), photo
  gallery at klevr.client-gallery.com, creator deck on Drive. No
  testimonial.
