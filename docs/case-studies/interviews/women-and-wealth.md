From the Iconic Events Brain (M165 Overview, Vision Brief, Call Log). Kept

- Internal code M165. Also called the Shelby Sapp Summit; the brain notes
  it is "not a She Sells event", it is Shelby Sapp's Women and Wealth
  Conference. Signed under the event's own company.
- Show days July 31 to August 2, 2026 (Friday to Sunday), load-in and
  setup July 29 to 30: five days on site.
- Broward County Convention Center, Fort Lauderdale, on the Intracoastal.
  Omni Hotel across the street as the primary hotel; Hilton Marina also
  approached.
- Planned for 2,000 to 2,500, capped at 2,500. Women only.
- Vision: the largest women in sales conference in the United States.
  "Tony Robbins meets a world-class women's summit, not a traditional
  sales conference." Production at Aspire Tour level, warmer and more
  feminine. Palette dark green, dark red, white and cream.
- Goals: establish Shelby as the definitive voice for women in sales
  across every industry; an experience so good attendees buy next year's
  ticket; break even, with any surplus going back into the experience.
- Identity: becoming the top 1% woman in your field. Sells a
  transformation, not a ticket. Content: psychology of sales,
  communication, negotiation, investing your income, mindset.
- Community design: a pre-event personality survey for every registrant,
  cohorts of 20 to 40 similar women, virtual cohort calls hosted by the
  Iconic team before the event, colour coded badges by cohort, so every
  attendee walks into day one already knowing her people.
- Roaring 20s gala, from Shelby's own Great Gatsby house party.
- An emcee who is not Shelby. A possible livestream for Women in Wealth
  Academy members.
- Team: Icon Becker (event director), Chrissy Belgin, Joanne Canoy, Pablo
  Varela (sponsor liaison). Six videographers and four photographers
  across every show day, twenty staff on registration and attendees.
- Launch planned for late May 2026, about two months out, with an inner
  circle ticket drop for Academy members first.
- A tentative 2027 date with the convention center was being drafted.
- Brain directive (Icon, 26 Oct 2026): the copy says we worked hand in
  hand with a local AV provider, neither taking credit for their work nor
  handing it all to them. No partner is named.
