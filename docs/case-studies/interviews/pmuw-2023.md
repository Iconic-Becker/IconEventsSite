## From the old site (row 5 of the legacy extract; Iconic's own copy)

- The PMU (permanent makeup) industry's largest live event was only about
  300 people. Danny wanted to blow it out of the water, and not run
  another boring PMU conference: workshop and business combined.
- Goal: the largest in the country, which meant passing 800 on site.
- Danny had never run an event this size and had no audience behind him.
  Five months to put it together. (The old page also gives the event
  cost: never published, house rule 2.)
- Media teased the event for months with no way to buy a ticket. Sales
  opened about 60 days out: 0 to about 400 in 45 days. Sponsors started
  coming to them. (The old page compares this to a famous failed festival:
  third party brand, left out.)
- Secured the Hard Rock Hotel on one of the most popular weekends of the
  year, through Iconic's network.
- Two stages run like a music festival: one for business, one for
  practice, so attendees could learn and do on site. Sponsor hall, VIP
  area.
- Installations built for selfies and content. South Florida installation
  companies could not produce them, so the team built them in house.
- About 600 on day one; almost 900 by the end. Metric on the old page:
  850 attendees. Largest PMU event in North America. $148,000 saved off
  contracts.
