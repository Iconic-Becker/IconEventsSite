# How Ben Newman Sold $1.8 Million in Memberships From the Stage at Casino Royale

*Compiled to the Iconic Events case study template. Live page: `/case-studies/casino-royale`*

---

## Summary

Ben Newman returned to live events after a five year gap with a new membership
tier to sell. Iconic Events, a Florida based event production and coordination
company, engineered a Casino Royale themed evening at Palms Casino Resort in Las
Vegas, projection mapped and choreographed so that the close was built into the
run of show rather than bolted onto the end of it. The room produced $1.8 million
in membership sales from 150 attendees, and the 2024 follow up event sold out
completely.

---

## The Challenge

Ben Newman had not held a live event in five years, and he was bringing a new
membership tier to an audience that already knew both him and his offer.
Familiarity was the problem: the room had to renew attention rather than
introduce anything. The new tier also needed a setting that made a high value
commitment feel proportionate to the decision being asked for.

---

## The Approach

Iconic Events held strategy, creative direction, production, show flow, and
on site execution under one team with a single line of accountability. The
engagement ran through the company's five phase method.

**Phase I, Insight (pre production)**
1. Defined the commercial outcome first: sell the new membership tier from the
   stage, not by follow up.
2. Mapped the audience's existing relationship to the host and the offer to
   locate where attention would drop.

**Phase II, Strategy (pre production)**
3. Set the room size and format against the commercial objective rather than a
   headcount target, landing on 150 seats.
4. Fixed the moment of the close, then sequenced every other decision backward
   from it.

**Phase III, Creative (pre production)**
5. Built a Casino Royale themed world as a single continuous environment, so the
   theme carried the evening instead of decorating it.
6. Designed the reveal of the membership tier as a staged moment inside that
   world.

**Phase IV, Production (on site)**
7. Projection mapped the venue to convert the Palms space into the themed build.
8. Choreographed the show flow so pacing, lighting, and stage transitions moved
   the audience toward the decision point.
9. Ran on site execution and show calling with the same team that designed the
   room.

**Phase V, Amplification (post event)**
10. Captured content through the evening for use after the close, including the
    following year's launch.
